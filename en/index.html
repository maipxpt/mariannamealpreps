hi, i'm marianna. i'm italian and brazilian, and i've loved cooking and helping people since i was young. this work lets me make a difference.

how it works

  1. i send you a menu of 5 to 6 dishes for the week
  2. we check together that the menu works for you
  3. i send a detailed grocery list, and the groceries are there the day i start
  4. one session of 5 to 7 hours at your place
  5. i wash the dishes and surfaces, label everything with the date and put it in the fridge and freezer

this is a weekly meal prep service. no plats à emporter, no big catering.

for people who struggle with, hate or don't want to cook: lack of time, disability, anything else.

i cook for 1, 2 or 4/5 people (a big family takes 7h). bread and pound cakes on request.

omnivore, vegetarian, vegan. i'm careful with allergies and sensitivities like lactose or gluten-free.

rates: write me. i accept payment with titres-services.

tuesdays, fridays and saturdays from 8:00. wednesdays only in woluwe, max 5h.
