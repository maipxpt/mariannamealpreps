hi, i'm marianna. i'm italian and brazilian, and i've loved cooking and helping people since i was young. this work lets me make a difference.

how it works

  1. i send you a menu of 5 to 6 dishes for the week
  2. we check together that the menu works for you
  3. i send a detailed grocery list, and the groceries are there the day i start
  4. one session of 5 to 7 hours at your place
  5. i wash the dishes and surfaces, label everything with the date and put it in the fridge and freezer

what it isa weekly meal prep service. no takeaway meals (plats à emporter), no big catering.

who it's forpeople who struggle with, hate or don't want to cook: lack of time, disability, anything else.

ratesaround €12 per hour with titres-services. first trial: €15 per hour.

whereall 19 communes of brussels.

whentuesdays, fridays and saturdays from 8:00. wednesdays only in woluwe, maximum 5 hours.

who i cook for1 to 6 people. families of 4 to 6 people take a 7-hour session, so i can prepare more portions. bread and pound cakes on request.

dietsomnivore, vegetarian, vegan. i'm careful with allergies and sensitivities like lactose or gluten-free.

questions

how much food do i get?

5 to 6 dishes per session (depending on the hours), 2 to 4 portions each. families of 4 to 6 people take a 7-hour session, so i can prepare more portions.

who buys the groceries?

you do. i send you a detailed grocery list, so you choose the food according to your budget and quality preferences.

do i need to be home?

it's advised for the first session. after that it's not necessary, and i work best with free access to the kitchen the whole time.

how long does the food keep?

up to 4 days in the fridge, and between 2 and 8 months in the freezer.

how do i cancel or reschedule?

by message, at least 24 hours in advance.

first, a kitchen visit

video chat or photos are free. in person is €5 for transport. i look at your equipment, pantry, fridge and freezer. secondhand is welcome, and you provide everything on the list, tupperware included.
